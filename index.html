BLASTORA

Privacy Policy · Last updated 28 October 2026

Blastora is a single-player block puzzle game published by Harshad Ladva. This policy explains what the game stores and what our advertising partner collects. It applies to the Blastora app on Android and iOS.

Information the game stores

Blastora works offline. Your best score for each mode and the number of games you have played are stored only on your device using the app's local preferences. They are not sent to us and are removed when you uninstall the app. The game has no account, login, chat, or user-generated content.

Advertising

Blastora shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier and other device identifiers;
  • IP address, used to infer approximate location;
  • app usage and ad interaction data (requests, impressions, clicks);
  • diagnostic and performance data about the ad SDK.

This data is used for advertising, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. Google handles it under the Google Privacy Policy and AdMob's data disclosure. We do not sell your data or share it with anyone else.

Your choices

EEA, UK, and Switzerland: on first launch you can accept or refuse personalised advertising through Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy options link on the game's home screen.

United States: where state privacy laws apply, the same consent tools let you opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

Children

Blastora is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will address it.

Data retention

Game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the app. Ad data is retained by Google according to its own retention policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
